1. Payment Terms

2. Delivery Timeline

  • Estimated delivery dates are best-faith estimates given the handmade nature of the garments — not guaranteed dates.

3. Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Deposits are non-refundable once paid — they secure fabric, artisan labour, and production capacity.

  • Cancellations must be made in writing (email accepted).

4. Alterations & Fit

  • Garments are made to measurements confirmed by the Client at the measurement appointment(s).
